BEHIND THE DATA

Transparent by design.

What we do

  1. Read official Y-9C and Call Report-derived inputs; reuse cached files and acquire missing inputs when available.
  2. Match institutions using identifiers and dated ownership. Use consolidated Y-9C balances without adding subsidiary balances again; retain independent banks and labelled bank-only aggregates.
  3. Add aggregated Call Report deposit detail separately, without adding it to consolidated deposit totals.
  4. Keep stocks at quarter-end. Derive quarterly flows from compatible YTD observations; withhold uncertain values.
  5. Validate, preserve provenance and export all 236 columns. Download the reports alongside a newly prepared dataset.

What to keep in mind

Predecessors remain separate before official merger dates. We do not claim an always-merged historical series.

Historical field-label harmonisation is limited to verified mappings. Missing mappings and unresolved ownership are flagged, not guessed.

Newly prepared results are research drafts and may require review. Uncertain quarterly flows are withheld and accounting conflicts are quarantined. Input failures and delivered-row checks are reported separately; missing-input checks are not passes. The frozen snapshot additionally applies ownership-completeness rules. Passing checks is not financial certification.

The free processing pilot runs on a local computer. It must remain awake and connected; availability is not guaranteed. One active job, one queued job, and bounded daily requests.
