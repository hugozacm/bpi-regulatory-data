# Provisional research assumptions

These working decisions are authorised as assumptions pending discussion with the research owner. They are not verified source facts or final research specifications.

- Initial extension: every calendar quarter of 2023–2025; expand earlier only after verifying source coverage and historical field definitions.
- Population: all available consolidated Y-9C filers plus relevant Call Report banks and savings institutions. Preserve entity types; do not restrict eligibility to BPI members. Banks with ambiguous ownership remain unresolved rather than being labelled standalone.
- Financial unit: USD thousands. Preserve raw source values and source-specific definitions.
- Core measures: assets, loans, equity, deposits, net income YTD and quarterly net income. Harmonise loan and equity definitions before combining sources for analysis.
- Detailed deposits: initially investigate noninterest-bearing versus interest-bearing, domestic versus foreign, and time-deposit categories. Enable a category only when its definition and period-valid source fields are verified. Do not invent mapping codes.
- Analytical perimeter: use consolidated Y-9C core measures where available. Aggregate selected Call Report deposit categories for the banks belonging to each holder at the report date. Preserve the deposit aggregation perimeter and reconcile differences from consolidated totals. Retain independently verified standalone banks separately.
- Flows: convert within the same calendar year using adjacent-quarter YTD values. Missing prior values remain missing. Flag structural or accounting changes affecting subtraction.
- Merger reference date: 31 December 2025. Preserve both as-reported and adjusted histories, with predecessor lineage and adjustment status.
- Merger scope: automatically adjust only verified full-entity combinations with sufficient predecessor data and validated accounting treatment. Partial deals, asset purchases, failed-bank purchases and ambiguous events require review; do not assume full predecessor transfer.
- Historical adjustments: do not add a predecessor separately once it is already consolidated into the successor. Flag unresolved intercompany eliminations, changing perimeters and missing predecessors; do not claim fully consolidated pro forma accounts.
- Acceptance: validate dates, completeness, raw uniqueness, ownership and output uniqueness; reconcile sample financials to official filings. Passing plausibility checks alone is not financial certification.
